# Design Briefs — 25 Landing Page Concepts

**Subject (constant across all 25):** a female fitness coach offering personalized coaching, training programs, motivation, physical transformation, wellness guidance, and online or in-person support.

**Deliverable per brief:** ONE cohesive single-page landing page — a single continuous scrolling experience — embodying the assigned design style. Each of the 25 styles is used exactly once. Order is randomized.

Each brief is exactly three paragraphs (the numbered heading and style name do not count as paragraphs). Briefs emphasize feeling, atmosphere, and abstract quality — not code, not specific brands or platforms.

---

## 1. Japandi (Japanese-Scandinavian fusion, zen meets hygge)

Design a single-page landing page for a female fitness coach whose positioning fuses disciplined Japanese restraint with warm Scandinavian comfort — a coaching concept built around mindful strength, sustainable habits, and the quiet confidence of a body cared for over years rather than weeks. Visitors should arrive into a feeling of exhaled calm: uncluttered space, natural light, a sense that nothing here will rush or shame them. As they scroll through this one cohesive page, the visual hierarchy should breathe — generous negative space, a low and steady rhythm — so the emotional flow feels like settling into a serene, ordered room where every element has earned its place. Incorporate colorful elements sparingly and intentionally — a single warm clay or muted sage accent against soft neutrals — so color reads as a deliberate emotional note rather than decoration.

The typography should feel gentle yet grounded — humanist, unhurried, quietly authoritative, the voice of someone who reassures without coddling. Interactions and animations should be soft and organic: slow fades, gentle easing, movements that feel like a deep breath rather than a click. The single-page journey should progress emotionally from a tranquil first impression, into an intimate introduction of the coach as a calm guide, through her method presented as a considered philosophy, her services and programs laid out with uncluttered clarity, her benefits framed as long-term wellbeing, her credibility shown through understated proof, and finally a call-to-action that invites rather than pushes — the whole arc a single, unbroken meditation on balance.

Draw abstract inspiration from the feeling of a tatami-floored room meeting a light-filled Nordic cabin: raw wood, linen, stone, the warmth of a hearth beside the discipline of a rock garden. Evoke the emotional qualities of a premium wellness retreat, of craftsmanship that hides its effort, of spaces designed for restoration rather than performance. Let these references shape a page whose sophistication comes from subtraction and material honesty — every choice should deepen the sense that strength and serenity are the same pursuit.
